Piano Practice — Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Piano Practice is an offline piano trainer for iPad, developed by Safronov Sviatoslav.

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, account system, or third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your note count, completed session count, best accuracy, and note-label preference locally in its own settings. The developer cannot access this information. No cloud-sync feature is provided. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple device settings.

Audio and permissions

The app generates sound on your device. It does not access the microphone, record audio, or listen to a physical instrument.

Deleting data

Delete the app from your iPad to remove its local app data. If you restore a device backup, that backup may restore earlier app data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from users of any age.

External links

Support and privacy links open external pages. Your browser and hosting provider process ordinary web requests under their own policies; the app does not attach practice data to these links.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published on this page. For privacy questions, email safronov.stas@gmail.com.
