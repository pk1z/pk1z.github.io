Fluffy Blocks — Support

A cozy falling-block puzzle for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

For help or feedback, email safronov.stas@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

How to play

Tap Let’s play. Move and rotate falling blocks to complete rows. Use the on-screen buttons, tap the board to rotate, swipe sideways to move one column, and swipe down to drop. The dotted outline previews the landing position. Complete rows disappear. Clear multiple rows together for more points. Every ten rows increases the level.

Pause and restart

Tap Pause to take a break. Leaving the app pauses play automatically. Start a new game from the pause screen. The current game is held in memory and is not restored after the app process closes.

Saved score

Your best score and haptic preference stay on your device. There is no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. Gameplay works offline. Support and privacy pages require internet access.
