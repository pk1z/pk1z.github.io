Fluffy Blocks — Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Fluffy Blocks is an offline puzzle game developed by Safronov Sviatoslav.

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party SDKs.

Local storage

Your best score and haptic preference are stored in the app’s own settings on your device. The developer cannot access these values. There is no cloud-sync feature. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple device settings.

Permissions

The app does not access your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, or location.

Deleting data

Delete the app to remove its local app data. Restoring a device backup may restore earlier app data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from users of any age.

External links

Support and privacy links open external web pages. Your browser and the hosting provider process ordinary web requests under their own policies. No gameplay data is attached to these links.

Contact and updates

Policy updates will appear here. For privacy questions, contact safronov.stas@gmail.com.
