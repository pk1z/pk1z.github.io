Piano Practice — Support

A calm place to learn notes, practice familiar melodies, and explore a two-octave keyboard on iPad.

Contact

For help or feedback, email safronov.stas@gmail.com. Include your iPad model, iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Getting started

Choose Learn a melody, select a lesson, and tap the indicated key. The lesson waits for the correct note. Hear it plays the remaining notes. Read notes starts a 20-note treble-clef exercise. Free play lets you explore the keyboard without scoring.

No sound?

Raise your iPad volume, check your selected Bluetooth or headphone output, and reopen the app. The tones are synthesized on the device.

Does it listen to my piano?

This release uses the on-screen keyboard. Microphone recognition, MIDI connections, and rhythm grading are not included.

Where is my progress?

Your practice statistics are saved on your device. There is no account or cloud-sync feature. Deleting the app removes its local app data.

Requirements

iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. Practice works offline. Opening support or privacy links requires internet access.
